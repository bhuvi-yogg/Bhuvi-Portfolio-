About

Building things, one prototype at a time

A quick look at where I'm coming from, what I'm learning, and how I like to work.

I'm currently in the 3rd semester of my BCA in Full Stack Web Development at Jaipur National University, Jaipur — after finishing my schooling in Dungarpur, Rajasthan.
